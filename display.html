Dear Ma'am Josue,

You may not remember me all and the students you taught throughout these years....

But I will remember you and carry these lessons you gave us for the rest of our lives.

The memories we shared, even only for a year, you made that year special.

You taught me that there's more than learning and cherish them in wellness or hardships,

And apply them to any situation we went through.

Guiding our way to the right path, to our lives we destined to be.

No matter how annoying, unruly, and naughty we are, you kept being patient with us.

You became the second parent we lean on when our own parents weren't there.

Thank you for everything you've done for us.

Science will always be my favorite subject because of the way you made it interesting and fun.

Thank you for being a great teacher and a great person.

-Sincerely, Marielle F. Mariano, SY: 2011-2012
